## Stretch

1. Two angles on a straight line are $x$ and $2x$. Find both angles.
2. Four angles around a point are $x$, $x$, $2x$ and $2x$. Find each angle.
3. Three angles meet at a point. One is a right angle and the other two are equal. How big are they?

{{< pagebreak >}}

## Transfer: angles on a clock face

A clock face is a full turn, shared out between 12 hours.

1. Through what angle does the hour hand turn in one hour? What is the angle between the hands at 4:00?
2. At 2:30 the minute hand points at the 6, but the hour hand is no longer on the 2. Where is it, and what is the angle between the hands?
3. A friend says the hands form a straight line at 9:15. Are they right? Explain.

*Hint for 2 and 3: the hour hand moves a little every minute, not just on the hour.*
