## Lesson

**Vertically opposite angles are equal.** When two straight lines cross, the angles facing each other match.

![](figures/crossing.svg){width=30%}

$a$ and $70^\circ$ each sit on a straight line with $b$, so both equal $180^\circ - b$. So $a = 70^\circ$ and $b = 110^\circ$.

**Parallel lines** never meet, like train rails; arrows mark them. A line crossing them, called a *transversal*, makes the same angles at both crossings.

![](figures/parallels.svg){width=78%}

- **Corresponding angles are equal.** They sit in the same position at each crossing: look for an **F** shape.
- **Alternate angles are equal.** They sit between the parallels, on opposite sides of the transversal: look for a **Z** shape.
