## Opener: the mind-reading trick

You are the audience. Follow these steps in your head or on scrap paper, and don't look at the bottom of the page yet.

> | Think of any whole number.
> | Add 5.
> | Double it.
> | Subtract 4.
> | Halve the result.
> | Take away the number you first thought of.

Got your answer? Now turn the page upside down.

- Try again with a different number: a big one, then a negative one. Does it still work?
- Can you find a number that breaks the trick?
- The sheet never knew your number. So how did it know your answer?

Inventors of tricks like this don't test thousands of numbers. They use algebra to prove the trick can never fail. By the end of this sheet you will do the same, and design a trick of your own.
