## Lesson

**Like terms** have the same letters raised to the same powers. Only the number in front (the *coefficient*) may differ.

Think of fruit: 3 apples + 2 apples = 5 apples. But 3 apples + 2 bananas stays as it is; you cannot call it 5 "bapples".

| Like terms | Not like terms |
|---|---|
| $4x$ and $-7x$ | $4x$ and $4y$ |
| $3ab$ and $ba$ | $x$ and $x^2$ |
| $2x^2$ and $-x^2$ | $5$ and $5x$ |

**To simplify**, group the like terms, then add their coefficients. The sign in front of a term travels with it.

- $4x + 3x = 7x$
- $5a + 2b - a = 4a + 2b$
- $3x^2 + 2x - x^2 + 5 = 2x^2 + 2x + 5$

**Watch out:** $x + x = 2x$, but $x \times x = x^2$.

2. $3ab + 2a - ab + 4b - 5a + 2ba$
3. $\tfrac{1}{2}x + \tfrac{1}{3}x$

## Transfer: the mind-reading trick

Ask a friend to do this in their head:

> Think of a number. Add 5. Double it. Subtract 4. Halve the result. Now take away the number you first thought of.

Then announce: "Your answer is 3." It always is.

1. Try the trick with three different starting numbers, including a negative one.
2. Call the starting number $n$. Write an expression for each step and simplify it. Why does $n$ disappear?
3. Invent your own trick that always ends in 7.

*Hint for 3: whatever you add early on, make sure you take away $n$ at the end.*
