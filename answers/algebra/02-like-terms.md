## Practice

1. $5y$
2. $6m + 2n$
3. $7k + 4$

## Stretch

1. $5x^2 + 4x - 3$
2. $4ab - 3a + 4b$ (since $ba = ab$)
3. $\tfrac{5}{6}x$

## Transfer

2. $n \to n + 5 \to 2n + 10 \to 2n + 6 \to n + 3 \to 3$. Halving undoes doubling, so subtracting $n$ removes every trace of the starting number.
3. Example: add 9, double, subtract 4, halve, subtract $n$: $n + 7 - n = 7$.
