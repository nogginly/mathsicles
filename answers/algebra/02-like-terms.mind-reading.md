## Stretch

## Back to the magician

3. $n \to n + 5 \to 2n + 10 \to 2n + 6 \to n + 3 \to 3$. Halving undoes the doubling, so taking away $n$ removes every trace of the starting number.
4. Always 5: $n + (n + 1) = 2n + 1 \to 2n + 10 \to n + 5 \to 5$.
5. Example: add 9, double, subtract 4, halve, take away $n$: $n + 7 - n = 7$. Any trick works if the $n$ terms cancel and the constants leave 7.
