## Stretch

1. $3x = 180^\circ$, so $x = 60^\circ$: the angles are $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$.
2. $6x = 360^\circ$, so $x = 60^\circ$: the angles are $60^\circ$, $60^\circ$, $120^\circ$ and $120^\circ$.
3. $360^\circ - 90^\circ = 270^\circ$, shared equally: $135^\circ$ each.

2. The hour hand is halfway between the 2 and the 3: $2.5 \times 30^\circ = 75^\circ$ from the 12. The minute hand is at $180^\circ$, so the angle is $105^\circ$.
