## Stretch

1. The angle alternate to one of them equals it, and sits on a straight line with the other. So the two add up to $180^\circ$.
2. $3x = x + 40^\circ$, so $x = 20^\circ$. Two angles are $60^\circ$; the other two are $120^\circ$.

## Back to the periscope

3. $45^\circ$ in and $45^\circ$ out leaves $180^\circ - 45^\circ - 45^\circ = 90^\circ$ between the beams: the light turns through $90^\circ$, straight down.
4. The light's path between the mirrors is a transversal across two parallel lines, so it meets the bottom mirror at the alternate angle, $45^\circ$. It leaves at $45^\circ$ too, turning $90^\circ$ back to horizontal: parallel to how it came in.
5. The alternate-angles step: it only works for parallel lines. With a tilted mirror the light leaves at a slant, so the view tilts up or down, and with a large tilt misses the window altogether.
