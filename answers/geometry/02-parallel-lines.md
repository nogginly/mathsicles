## Stretch

1. The angle alternate to one of them equals it, and sits on a straight line with the other. So the two add up to $180^\circ$.
2. $3x = x + 40^\circ$, so $x = 20^\circ$. Two angles are $60^\circ$; the other two are $120^\circ$.
3. No: parallel lines would give equal corresponding angles. The lines lean towards each other on the side of the smaller interior angles, so they meet on that side of the transversal. (Drawing it is the clearest check.)

## Transfer

1. The sun's rays are parallel, and the line from the centre through Alexandria (and up the stick) crosses them. The angle at the stick and the angle at the centre are alternate angles, so both are $7.2^\circ$.
2. $\frac{7.2}{360} = \frac{1}{50}$.
3. $50 \times 800 = 40\,000$ km. The modern figure is about $40\,000$ km round the poles.
