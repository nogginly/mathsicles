## Stretch

1. $3x = 180^\circ$, so $x = 60^\circ$: the angles are $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$.
2. $6x = 360^\circ$, so $x = 60^\circ$: the angles are $60^\circ$, $60^\circ$, $120^\circ$ and $120^\circ$.

## Back to the bees

3. $360^\circ \div 3 = 120^\circ$.
4. Squares: $360^\circ \div 90^\circ = 4$. Triangles: $360^\circ \div 60^\circ = 6$. Both divide 360° exactly, so they fit with no gaps.
5. No. Three pentagon corners make $324^\circ$, leaving a $36^\circ$ gap; four make $432^\circ$, which overlap. $108^\circ$ does not divide $360^\circ$ exactly.
