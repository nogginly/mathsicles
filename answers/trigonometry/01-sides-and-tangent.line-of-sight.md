## Stretch

1. Yes: $\frac{6}{8} = \frac{9}{12} = 0.75$. Same ratio means same angle.
2. Opposite $= 0.5 \times 10 = 5$ cm.

## Back to the archer

3. $\tan = \frac{4}{12} = \frac{1}{3}$
4. The line must drop 2 m more at the same slope: $2 \div \frac{1}{3} = 6$ m. The rogue is hidden within 6 m of the wall.
5. Slope $= \frac{h - w}{d}$, so the strip is $w \div \frac{h - w}{d} = \frac{w\,d}{h - w}$. Check: $\frac{2 \times 12}{6 - 2} = 6$ m. (If $h \le w$, everything behind the wall is hidden.)
