## Practice

1. Hypotenuse $p$, opposite $r$, adjacent $q$.
2. Hypotenuse $p$, opposite $q$, adjacent $r$.
3. $\tan\theta = \frac{6}{8} = 0.75$

## Stretch

1. Yes: $\frac{6}{8} = \frac{9}{12} = 0.75$. Same ratio means same angle (the triangles are similar).
2. Opposite $= 0.5 \times 10 = 5$ cm. Hypotenuse $= \sqrt{5^2 + 10^2} = \sqrt{125} \approx 11.2$ cm.
3. $\tan\varphi = \frac{8}{6} \approx 1.33$, the reciprocal of $\tan\theta$. Always true: opposite and adjacent swap, so the fraction flips.

## Transfer

1. Opposite: the stick (1 m) or the pyramid's height. Adjacent: each shadow.
2. $\tan = \frac{1}{1.6} = 0.625$. Height $= 0.625 \times 232 = 145$ m. (The real pyramid was about 147 m when built.)
